## 📊 Advanced Features (190+)

### What it analyzes:
- **Pitch** - How steady is the voice?
- **Jitter** - Pitch irregularity (PD indicator)
- **Shimmer** - Amplitude variation (PD indicator)
- **HNR** - Voice quality (Harmonic-to-Noise Ratio)
- **Spectral** - Frequency content ("colors" of voice)
- **MFCC** - Perceptual features of speech
- **Energy** - Overall loudness variations
- **Statistics** - Advanced distribution analysis

**Total**: 215 professional features analyzed simultaneously
